pennywhiskers

a tiny cat holds your fun money for the day and never shames you when you slip.

coming soon to the app storefor iphone · pay once

no bank. no sign-up. no ads.one gold coin every morning, one tap to log a spend, and a friend who is sad, not mad, on the hard days. your money story is one file on your phone: export it any time, delete the app and it is gone.

pennywhiskers is a game about noticing your own spending. it is not financial advice, it does not connect to your bank, and it never touches real money.
