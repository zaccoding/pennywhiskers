Pennywhiskers Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

Your money, your cat, and everything you type stay on your phone. Pennywhiskers has no account, no sign up, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no server. We collect nothing, from anyone of any age.

Where your data lives

Your save is one file in the app's private storage on your device, shared only with the app's widget. Deleting the app deletes it. Settings has Export, which gives you the file, and Erase All Data, which deletes it.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, iOS schedules them on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app's update notes will say so.
